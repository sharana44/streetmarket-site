How it works

  1. Find a shop

    See shops near you on the map, with their listings and prices.

  2. Order ahead

    Place your order from your phone and follow its status as the shop works on it.

  3. Collect and pay

    We notify you when it's ready. Pick it up and pay the shop directly.

  • Share a cart. Build an order together with family or flatmates.
  • Save your favourites. Reorder from the shops you use most in a couple of taps.
  • No cards needed. The app never asks for card or bank details.

For shops and stalls

Run your counter from your phone.

Customers pay you directly at pickup. StreetMarket takes no cut of the order and never handles your money.

  • Put your listings online with photos, prices and categories
  • Get an alert the moment a customer orders
  • Mark items unavailable in one tap when you run out
  • Use pickup codes to hand the right order to the right customer
