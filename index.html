Order ahead from the shops around you.

StreetMarket has two free apps. Customers use StreetMarket to order from nearby shops and collect. Shops use StreetMarket Partner to receive and prepare those orders.

For customers

StreetMarket

Find shops near you, order from your phone and pick it up when it's ready. No queue, no phone call, no wasted trip.

  • See nearby shops on a map with their items, prices and opening hours
  • Order ahead and follow every step until it's ready
  • Get a notification and a pickup code when you can collect
  • Pay the shop directly. The app never asks for card or bank details
  • Share a cart with family or flatmates and reorder favourites

For shops and stalls

StreetMarket Partner

Put your shop online and take orders from your phone. Customers pay you directly at pickup. We take no cut and never handle your money.

  • Add your products with photos, prices, sizes and offers
  • Get an alert the moment a customer orders, then accept and mark it ready
  • Set opening hours for each day and mark items out of stock in one tap
  • Hand over the right order with pickup codes
  • Add staff so your team can manage orders too

How it works

If you're buying

  1. Find a shop. Browse shops near you and what they sell.
  2. Order ahead. Place your order and watch the shop work on it.
  3. Collect and pay. We tell you when it's ready. Pay the shop at pickup.

If you run a shop

  1. Register your shop. Add your location, hours and products.
  2. Accept orders. Your phone alerts you; accept and prepare.
  3. Hand over and get paid. Check the pickup code and take payment directly.
